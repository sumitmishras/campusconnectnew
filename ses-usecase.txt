Campus Connect is a private student community application for Chandigarh University, India.

WHAT WE SEND
Transactional email only. The single message type is a one-time 8-digit sign-in code, sent to a student's own email address at the moment they type that address into the app and press "send code". We also send account-security notices (email change, account deletion confirmation). We send no marketing, no newsletters, no promotional content of any kind.

WHO RECEIVES IT
Only people who typed their own address into our app seconds earlier. We do not have, buy, rent, import or scrape recipient lists. Sign-up is additionally restricted to university email addresses, enforced both client-side and by a database trigger, so an address outside the university domain cannot even request a code.

VOLUME
Under 500 messages per day expected at launch, with a peak during semester registration. Each message is a direct response to a user action; there is no bulk or scheduled sending path in the system.

BOUNCE AND COMPLAINT HANDLING
An SES configuration set named "campus-connect" is attached to the sending identity as its default. It publishes BOUNCE, COMPLAINT, REJECT and RENDERING_FAILURE events to an SNS topic that is monitored. Account-level suppression is enabled for BOUNCE and COMPLAINT, so a hard-bounced or complaining address is automatically suppressed from future sends. Reputation metrics are enabled on the configuration set.

UNSUBSCRIBE
Because the only mail we send is a code the recipient requested moments before, there is no mailing list to unsubscribe from - a student stops receiving mail by not requesting a code. Students can delete their account from within the app, which removes their address from our database after a 30-day grace period.

INFRASTRUCTURE NOTE
The service runs on EC2 in us-east-1 in this account. The website URL given is the application's live API endpoint on a temporary IP-based hostname; a permanent domain is being registered and we will verify it as a domain identity with DKIM once it is in place.
